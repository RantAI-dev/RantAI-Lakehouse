## Why

At QA on 2026-11-14 the owner found the Dashboards page (`/dashboards/browse`) busy:
- 11 controls of mixed styles share one toolbar row;
- the badges disagree (Public in orange, Embed in grey);
- the built-in Main card has no ⋯ menu;
- every card says "No description.";
- two chip rows (Favourites, Recent) sit above folder groups;
- the assistant bar covers the content.

Of three mockups the owner chose **C, Home and table**, with **B's gallery** (pictures of each
dashboard's first tiles) as a second view.

## Targets

| # | Target | Best competitor | Ours today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | One primary action ("New") and at most three other visible controls in the page header and toolbar; everything else in one ⋯ menu | Looker Studio: one "Create" plus search | 11 controls in one row |
| T2 | One badge style, with one colour per meaning (built-in, public link, embedded, restricted) | Metabase: one verified-badge style | two styles, colours disagree |
| T3 | Dashboards, reports and templates in one list, filtered by a segmented control | Metabase collections list every item type together | three separate tabs |
| T4 | The gallery view shows each dashboard as a picture of its tiles, drawn from the chart definitions (kind and position) with no query: one request for the whole list. Pictures with the real numbers need an API that reads only remembered results (`BI-60`) | Tableau shows real thumbnails; Metabase shows none | no thumbnails |
| T5 | At 1190×800 nothing overlaps and the assistant bar covers no row; light and dark; phone width stacks with no horizontal scroll | All three | the assistant bar covers rows |

*(proposed)*: T1's limit of three other controls. The owner confirms at QA.
