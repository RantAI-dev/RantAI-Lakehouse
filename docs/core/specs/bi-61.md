## Why

At QA on 2026-11-14 the owner found the page that shows one dashboard (`/dashboards/<id>`) busy:
- the header carries six controls of mixed styles (Edit layout, star, ⋯, Comments, Parameters,
  New chart);
- the ⋯ menu lists 22 flat entries, seven of them for auto-refresh;
- every tile wears a blue bordered "Manual" badge;
- tiles are bordered boxes;
- the parameter and filter bar is a bordered box;
- the assistant bar covers the bottom tiles.

Of three mockups the owner chose **A, Focused reader**. They liked B's side panel, but it squeezed
the tiles once the AI Copilot panel also opened, hence the rule that only one side panel shows
at a time.

## Targets

| # | Target | Best competitor | Ours today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Reading mode shows at most three header controls: Edit (pencil), **Share** (primary) and ⋯ | Looker Studio view mode: Share, Edit, ⋯ | six |
| T2 | The ⋯ menu is grouped (View, Get a copy, Manage), with auto-refresh as one submenu: at most 14 top-level entries | Metabase: about 8 | 22 flat |
| T3 | No per-tile badge in reading mode; freshness and refresh mode live in the tile ⋯ and a hover line | Tableau: none | one badge on every tile |
| T4 | One side panel at a time: Comments and Details open in the same right panel as the AI Copilot, as tabs | n/a | two panels can open together |
| T5 | At 1190×800 and 1366×900 nothing overlaps and the composer covers no tile; light and dark; phone stacks with no horizontal scroll | All three | the composer covers tiles |
