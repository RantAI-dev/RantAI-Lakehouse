## Why

Without a fixed test set, the assistant can silently get worse when a tool changes.

## Decisions (product owner, 2026-10-13 and 2026-10-15)

- **At least 30 typical requests** with expected results, and the set grows by
  at least one per AI item. Signed 2026-10-13.
- **At least 90% pass**, and no request that passed before may fail. Signed
  2026-10-13.
- The set runs against a **throwaway local stack**, never staging.
- **Not in CI** (2026-10-15): the run needs a model key and builds an image, so
  a person runs it, with one command, on every change to an assistant tool or
  prompt.

## What users get

Confidence that the assistant does not regress.
