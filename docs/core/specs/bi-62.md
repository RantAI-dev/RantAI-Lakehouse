## Why

`BI-18` part D (#528) shipped reports: a title and an ordered list of blocks (heading,
paragraph, list, link, chart) edited as a stack of bordered form cards, one textarea per block,
up/down/trash buttons, an "Add block" menu and a Save button. At QA on 2026-11-15 the owner asked
for a page that feels like writing in Notion instead of filling a form ("pas create report ini
tampilannya bisa kek langsung rich text editor gitu kah? kayak notion gitu? kalo sekarang kan
bentukannya kek form form gitu"). Of four mockups the owner chose **D**, which combines a bare
document (A), a chart shelf in the side panel (B) and inline help from the assistant (C): "aku
suka yang D ini, gaskan kita implementasikan".

## Targets

| # | Target | Best competitor | Ours today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | Writing happens on the page: the title is the first line, text has no boxes or block labels, and a report reads the same in the editor and in the reader | Notion | form cards with labels |
| T2 | Every insert is reachable from the keyboard: `/` menu (headings, lists, quote, divider, chart, write with AI), Markdown shortcuts (`#`, `##`, `-`, `1.`, `>`, `---`), ⌘B/⌘I/⌘K, Alt+↑/↓ to move a block | Notion | an "Add block" menu only |
| T3 | Bold, italic and links inside a sentence | Notion, Metabase Documents | plain text; links are separate blocks |
| T4 | Charts are picked from live previews (the shelf in the side panel and the picker), dragged or clicked in, and can be text-width or wide | Metabase Documents (card picker), Tableau Stories | a text list in a dialog, one width |
| T5 | No Save button: changes are saved about 1 s after typing stops, with a visible Saving… / Saved / Couldn't save · Retry state; nothing typed is lost on a refresh within that second | Notion, Google Docs | explicit Save |
| T6 | The PDF and the reader page show the same blocks, marks and chart widths as the editor | Metabase Documents | same, for today's blocks |
| T7 | The assistant writes in the page: "Ask AI to write…" and "Describe" on a chart stream a draft that is accepted or discarded, and the same exchange shows in the AI Copilot tab | Notion AI, Hex Magic | none in the editor |
| T8 | Light and dark, 1190×800 and 390×844 with no sideways scroll, reduced motion respected, focus visible | all | partly |

## Not in this item

- Real-time co-editing, comments on a report, version history for reports (still listed under
  `BI-18` part D "Not included").
- Images, tables or embeds inside a report other than charts.
- Scheduled delivery of a report (`RPT-1` covers dashboards).
