## Why

The create and edit chart page (`BI-6`, `WEB-7` S2) puts three narrow columns side by
side: a long list of chart types, five accordion sections of equal weight, and a preview
that stays empty until a source is picked. At a laptop width the header row overlaps
("Title" and "Dashboard"), and the assistant bar covers controls. At QA on 2026-11-14 the
owner found the spacing, hierarchy, padding and visibility lacking. Of three mockups, the
owner chose **A, Canvas**. They also asked to keep **B's chart-type previews drawn from
the person's own data**, because people would rather see than read.

## Targets

| # | Target | Best competitor | Ours today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | The preview is the largest area of the page, at least half its width at 1366×900 | Tableau, Looker Studio: the canvas is the page | about a quarter, empty until a source is picked |
| T2 | At 1190×800 and 1366×900, no control overlaps another, and the assistant bar covers none | All three | header labels overlap, and the assistant bar covers the type list and settings |
| T3 | Choosing the chart type shows each type as a small picture of the person's data | Looker Studio shows icons; none draws the data | a text list with truncated names |
| T4 | From an empty page to a first chart preview: pick a source, then one click on a column for X and one for the value (3 actions) | Metabase: pick a table, then summarise by (about 4) | source, open "What to show", pick X, pick measure, pick aggregate (5 or more) |
| T5 | Every setting the builder has today is still reachable, and a saved chart is byte-for-byte the same spec | n/a | n/a |
| T6 | Light and dark mode; at phone width the page stacks with no horizontal scroll | All three | not checked |
